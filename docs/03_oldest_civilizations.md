# Oldest Civilisations - Who, Where, How Long

Using classic criteria (cities, writing, government, intensive agriculture):

## 1. Sumer - Southern Mesopotamia (Southern Iraq)
- First known complex civilization, developed first city-states in 4th millennium BCE
- Flourished between circa 4000 and 1750 BCE. Also given as c.4500-1900 BCE, or 4100-1750 BC.
- Control lasted short of 2,000 years before Babylonians took charge in 2004 BC
- Duration as distinct Sumerian culture: ~2,000-2,300 years. Mesopotamian civilisation continues after under Akkadians, Babylonians, Assyrians.

## 2. Ancient Egypt - Nile Valley
- Unification ~3100 BCE, hieroglyphic writing emerging 3100-2560 BCE
- Sumerians ~4000 BC, Egyptians ~3200 BC, Indus ~2600 BC in rough order
- Longest-lived: Egypt enduring more than 3,000 years until Cleopatra's death made it Roman province (30 BCE). ~3070 years.

## 3. Indus Valley - Pakistan / NW India
- Era c.3300 BCE – 1300 BCE, developed from at least 3300 BCE
- Cities: Harappa and Mohenjo-daro featuring sophisticated urban planning with grid streets and drainage
- Duration ~2000 years

## 4. Caral-Supe / Norte Chico - Peru
- c.3000-2600 BCE, pyramids rising in Supe Valley, completely independent of any outside influence
- Duration ~1200 years as monumental centre

## 5. Early China - Yellow River
- Xia Dynasty traditionally c.2070 BCE, Erlitou culture ~1900 BCE
- Culturally continuous to present as tradition, though dynasties change.

All these areas had indigenous hunter-gatherer populations much earlier. Civilisations evolved from them locally, not via invasion in most modern interpretations.
