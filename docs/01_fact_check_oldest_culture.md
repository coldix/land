# Fact Check: "World's Oldest Culture / Civilisation"

Original post from Save Australian History & Culture argued:
- Civilisation has a specific meaning (cities, writing, big buildings, government, farming surplus)
- Aboriginal societies were complex hunter-gatherers with kinship, oral tradition, fire management, art
- Evidence supports one of longest continuous cultures *outside Africa* - qualifier often left out
- Genetic studies show arrival ~50kya
- Continuous doesn't mean unchanged
- San people of southern Africa have older genetic roots

**Verdict: Substantially accurate with important nuance.**

### Civilisation definition
Traditional definition: complex society characterized by urban development, social stratification, form of government, and symbolic systems like writing. Literal definition "society made up of cities". Also includes agriculture, division of labour, urban centres, literate writing system, standards of measurement.

Aboriginal pre-1788 societies don't meet classic civilisation checklist, but do meet culture.

### Oldest continuous culture
Australian Museum and similar institutions say 65,000 years - world's oldest continuous living culture. Academic source often quoted: ancestors left Africa 64-75kya, inhabited same territory continuously longer than any other populations outside Africa.

Other summary: San people of Southern Africa are direct descendants of earliest populations, while Aboriginal Australians are considered oldest civilization outside Africa.

### Dates
Synthesis: arrival 50,000–65,000 years ago, with strong support for 65k at Madjedbebe rock shelter. Maternal lineages show ~50k. Genetic studies support 50-70k.

### Africa point
San peoples are members of indigenous hunter-gatherer cultures of southern Africa, oldest surviving cultures of region. Some interpretations suggest divergence 100-200kya. San are direct descendants of first Homo sapiens. San directly descended from original groups 100-140kya, Aboriginal ancestors became distinctive group ~50kya.

Conclusion: Post is correct to separate culture vs civilisation and to add "outside Africa".
