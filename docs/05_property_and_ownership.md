# When Could It Be Called Civilisation? When Did Property Become Saleable?

## Civilisation threshold
- Emergence of Civilization in Sumer c.3100-2800 BC. By 3100 BC people living in cities and had developed preponderance of elements constituting civilization. First evidence of writing, so called Protoliterate period.
- World's earliest known texts from Uruk and Jemdet Nasr date between c.3350-2500 BC.

So ~3350-3100 BCE is when Sumer becomes civilisation in classic sense.

## Property evolution

### Stage 1: Temple/community owns all (c.4000-3000 BCE)
- Sumerian temple considered territory of state as its own, regarding itself as actual unlimited owner. No one else could dispose except them. Temple transferred right of use as land lease or loan. Not a single real sale and purchase among many thousands of early records.

Use-rights based on membership, not purchase.

### Stage 2: First private sales, rare (c.3000-2300 BCE)
- Earliest recorded real estate transactions traced to ~3000 BCE where clay tablets documented land sales
- Sumerian Tablet, Real Estate Circa 3000 BC exists
- Stele of Ushumgal commemorates Sumerian real estate transaction around 2800 BCE - religious authorities seen as owner of most property, but shows at least some land privately owned
- Sumerian tablet c.2350 BC documenting land sale
- Geographic difference: larger holdings and more extensive private land in northern Babylonia, far less private land in south (Lagash, Uruk), more extensive temple estates in south
- Even when sale allowed, ancient kudurrus (2300-2100 BCE) show for a field to be sold all male members of extended family had to consent - keeping land within family
- Climate stress between end of Uruk (3300-3100 BC) and Jemdet Nasr/Early Dynastic (3100-2550 BC) reduced returns, forced religious elites to share power, led to peasants engaging in leasing, renting, tenure-for-service contracts

### Stage 3: Private transferable property becomes normal (c.2100-1600 BCE)
- Ur III period, sale documents became more common on clay tablets. Real estate (houses, orchards) main object.
- Contract for Sale of Real Estate, Sumer c.2000 BC - transaction from last days of Sumerian history, with description, house, neighbours.
- Formula modernizes: scribes record identities of parties, description, price, witnesses including surveyor
- By 1600s BC leasing and selling of privately owned land was common, silver default medium, some sale documents even included warranties against title defects requiring seller to appear in court
- Private holders deal with them as they please - sell, let out, divide and testate as if private property

### Summary
- Civilisation: ~3300-3100 BCE
- First ownable/sellable land examples: ~2800-3000 BCE exceptional
- Common market: ~2350-2000 BCE becoming common, fully established by ~1600 BCE
- ~1000 years between first cities and fully transferable private land.

This contrasts with Aboriginal Country system which maintained use-rights managed by kin/law/fire, not commodified sale for silver - similar to early Sumerian temple stage.
