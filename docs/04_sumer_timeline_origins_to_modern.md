# Sumer Area Timeline - Origins to Modern Day
# Southern Mesopotamia / Southern Iraq

## Deep Origins
- c.100,000-12,000 BCE - Hunter-gatherers in Tigris-Euphrates, Zagros foothills
- c.10,000-6500 BCE - Pre-Pottery Neolithic, first villages in north (Jarmo), farming spreads south

## Foundation of Sumer (indigenous evolution)
- c.6500-4100 BCE - **Ubaid period** - First villages in far south: Eridu, Ur, Ubaid. Irrigation, temples. Population called Proto-Euphrateans/Ubaidians, theorized to have evolved from Samarra culture of northern Mesopotamia.
- c.4100-2900 BCE - **Uruk period** - First cities, Uruk 40k+ people, invention of writing c.3400 BCE, no traces of invasion in transition from Ubaid to Uruk - unbroken sequence of temples in Eridu.
- c.3100-2900 BCE - **Jemdet Nasr** - Transitional
- c.2900-2334 BCE - **Early Dynastic** - City-states: Uruk, Ur, Lagash, Shuruppak. Kings, Gilgamesh.
- c.2334-2154 BCE - **Akkadian Empire** - Sargon, first multi-ethnic empire. Period 2334-2218 BCE.
- c.2193-2119 BCE - Gutian interlude
- c.2112-2004 BCE - **Ur III** - Third Dynasty of Ur, last Sumerian renaissance, ziggurat at Ur. Ends 2004 BC.

## After Sumerian language dies as spoken
- c.2004-1763 BCE - Isin-Larsa, Amorite dynasties, Sumerian now liturgical
- c.1894-1595 BCE - Old Babylonian, Hammurabi
- c.1595-1155 BCE - Kassite Babylon
- c.1155-911 BCE - Second Dynasty of Isin, rising Assyria
- c.911-609 BCE - Neo-Assyrian Empire dominates
- c.626-539 BCE - Neo-Babylonian, Nebuchadnezzar II
- 539-331 BCE - Achaemenid Persian
- 331-141 BCE - Alexander / Seleucid
- 141 BCE-224 CE - Parthian
- 224-651 CE - Sassanian, capital at Ctesiphon near old Sumer
- 651-750 CE - Islamic conquest, Umayyad
- 762-1258 CE - Abbasid, Baghdad founded 762 CE 90km north of Babylon with bricks from Babylon
- 1258-1534 CE - Mongols, etc., irrigation damaged, marshes expand
- 1534-1918 CE - Ottoman, Basra/Baghdad provinces, Marsh Arabs Ma'dan maintain life in old marshes
- 1920-1932 - British Mandate of Mesopotamia
- 1932-present - Modern Iraq, independence 1932, republic 1958, Ba'ath 1968-2003, Federal Iraq 2003-present. Sites Eridu, Ur, Uruk in Dhi Qar/Muthanna Governorates.

Key point: Sumer flourished c.4000-1750 BCE, but grew from local Ubaid villages since 6500 BCE. Area continuously inhabited ~8500 years of farming settlement.
