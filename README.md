# Land - Culture, Civilisation, Property and Australian Land Tenure

This repo documents a discussion that started from a Facebook post claiming Aboriginal Australians have the world's oldest culture/civilisation, and expanded into a broader history of how civilisation, property, and land tenure evolved in Mesopotamia and Australia.

## What's Inside

**01_fact_check_oldest_culture.md**
Fact-check of the original Save Australian History & Culture post. Distinguishes culture vs civilisation, checks the "outside Africa" qualifier, genetic dates 50-65kya, and the San peoples as older genetic lineage.

**02_fire_management.md**
Is calling it "management" a stretch? Covers Rhys Jones's 1969 "firestick farming" term, hunting vs mosaic burning, low-intensity burns preventing wildfires, and why it's management but not agriculture in the European sense.

**03_oldest_civilizations.md**
Who/where were the oldest civilisations and how long did they last? Sumer (c.4000-1750 BCE), Egypt (3100-30 BCE ~3070 years), Indus Valley (3300-1300 BCE), Caral-Supe Peru (3000-1800 BCE), early China.

**04_sumer_timeline_origins_to_modern.md**
Full timeline for the Sumer area - from pre-Ubaid hunter-gatherers, Ubaid 6500-4100 BCE, Uruk 4100-2900 BCE, Early Dynastic, Akkadian, Ur III, Old Babylonian, Kassite, Assyrian, Neo-Babylonian, Persian, Greek, Parthian, Sassanian, Islamic, Ottoman, British Mandate, to modern Iraq. Emphasis on indigenous evolution, not invasion.

**05_property_and_ownership.md**
Two questions: when could Sumer be called a civilisation (~3350-3100 BCE with cities and writing) and when did property become ownable and transferable? Three stages: temple owns all (4000-3000 BCE), first rare private sales ~2800 BCE (Stele of Ushumgal, tablets c.3000 BCE, c.2350 BCE), sale documents common by Ur III c.2100-2000 BCE, fully common by 1600 BCE with Torrens-like details (parties, description, price, witnesses, surveyor).

**06_australia_crown_and_private_land.md**
Australia from Country to Crown land to private land. 1770 Cook claims eastern coast under terra nullius, 1788 First Fleet - all land vested in Crown. 1792 first private grants. 1826 Limits of Location, 1830s squatters on Crown land, 1835 Batman treaty voided - only Crown can grant. 1847 pastoral leases (14-year Crown leases). 1858 Torrens Title invented in SA - Real Property Act 1858, first sale 25 Aug 1858, all colonies adopt 1858-1875. 1861 Robertson Land Acts NSW - Crown Lands Alienation Act and Occupation Act - free selection before survey, 40-320 acres at £1/acre, breaking squatter monopoly. 1901 Federation. 1992 Mabo v Queensland (No.2) 3 June 1992 overturns terra nullius, first recognition of indigenous property rights, leading to Native Title Act 1993.
